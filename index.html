How we work

Five steps, in this order.

Booking a discovery call does not commit you to buying anything.

  1. 1

    Initial enquiry

    You tell us about your business, its stage, how long it has operated and what you need help with.

  2. 2

    Discovery conversation

    A 30-minute conversation to understand your business, current challenges and the outcome you want. It is not an audit, and there is no obligation to buy.

  3. 3

    Assessment and proposal

    We summarise what we understand, recommend an approach (it may be one of the options below), define the scope and send a fee proposal.

  4. 4

    Build and implementation

    Once you accept the proposal, we develop and implement the agreed solution.

  5. 5

    Training and handover

    We help the right people learn the system and hand over the agreed documentation and resources.

Giftie Obeten

That interest led me into operations, where I found myself drawn to the work that happens behind the scenes: how people work together, how decisions become action, and how the right systems can make running a business easier.

Questions

Fair things to ask.

Do I need to know what is wrong before we talk?

No. You don't need it all figured out. Tell us where you are and what keeps going wrong, and we take it from there.

Do I have to buy something after the discovery call?

No. The call is a 30-minute conversation to understand your business. If we go further, we send an assessment and a fee proposal, and you decide whether to accept it.

What is the difference between the discovery call and the Diagnostic?

The discovery call is a 30-minute conversation where we learn about your business and what you want to change. The Diagnostic is one of the three ways to work with us: a review of how your business runs today, with a gap and friction analysis, written recommendations and a priority action roadmap. We propose its scope and fee after the call, and you decide whether to go ahead.

Do you only give advice?

No. We build the systems and hand them over, with training and documentation for the people who will use them.

What will it cost?

It depends on the scope, so we don't publish prices. After the discovery conversation we propose a scope and a fee.

Which tools do you use?

That depends on your business and what you already use. It can include no-code tools, automation platforms and conventional software. The Business OS is built in Notion where it fits.

How quickly will you reply?

We respond to every enquiry within 24 hours of receiving it.
